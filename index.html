The curriculum

Six weeks built around your next level.

Learning design is our strongest muscle. Every session is intentional — structured to build on the last, designed to create real shifts, not just interesting discussions.

Week 01

Mapping the impact tech landscape

Who the players are, how the sector is structured, and how to find where your background and interests connect to work that matters.

Week 02

Knowing your own value

Skills audit, transferable experience, naming what you genuinely bring. Not a confidence exercise — a rigorous look at what you actually have to offer.

Week 03

Confidence, barriers & taking up space

The internal blocks that slow women down in male-dominated sectors. We name them, work through them, and build strategies to move anyway.

Week 04

Building a network that works for you

How relationships actually open doors. Practical, uncringe approaches to building visibility and genuine connection in this sector.

Week 05

Telling your story with authority

Translating your experience into language that lands — in interviews, applications, and every conversation where you need to be seen clearly.

Week 06

Your concrete next 90 days

Leave with a specific, actionable plan and accountability structures. The programme ends — the momentum does not have to.

Common questions

Honest answers.

I don't feel "senior enough" to apply. Should I?

Yes. This programme is specifically designed for women who feel that gap — between where they are and where they want to be. That feeling is exactly what we work through together.

What is "impact tech" exactly?

Organisations where technology actively drives positive change — climate tech, civic tech, digital health, EdTech, sustainable finance, social enterprise. We help you find where your background and interests connect.

Do I need a technical background?

No. Impact organisations need product managers, communicators, strategists, designers, policy specialists, and operators. This is not a programme just for engineers.

How much time does it take each week?

Plan for 4–6 hours. Live sessions, community engagement, and guided in between-session reflection. We won't ask for more than that.

I'm based in the Global South. Is this relevant to me?

Yes — impact tech is a global sector. We actively welcome participants from outside the UK and Europe, and scholarship places are specifically available. The programme is fully online.

Will this get me a new job?

We won't make that promise — it would be dishonest. What it gives you is clarity, confidence, positioning, and connections to make your next step significantly more achievable. Past participants have found new jobs through the mentorship.
